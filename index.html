          Questions
          What makes a password strong?

          At least 12 characters, a mix of A–Z, a–z, 0–9 and symbols, and no names, years, common words or patterns like 123.

          Why is “Rahul@2004” weak?

          A name followed by a year is one of the first patterns hackers try. The symbol does not help much.

          What is entropy?

          A measure of how hard a password is to guess, in bits. Each extra bit doubles the guesses needed. 60+ bits is strong.

          How do I remember strong passwords?

          Use the Memorable button: five random words with a symbol and numbers. Or use a trusted password manager.

          What is personal data?

          Anything that identifies you: name, phone, address, Aadhaar, photos, birth date. India’s DPDP Act, 2023 protects it, but you must protect it too.

          What is masked Aadhaar?

          An Aadhaar copy showing only the last 4 digits. Download it from the official UIDAI myAadhaar website.

          Is my password sent anywhere?

          No. Everything runs on your device, even without internet.
